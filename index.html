Behind the work

A closer look

I am a freelance IT technician, software developer, entrepreneur, and mobile barber with a passion for understanding how things work and finding practical ways to make them work better.

My experience spans computer and mobile device troubleshooting, hardware and software solutions, networking, web development, application development, and technical support.

Beyond technical support, I am developing software products and digital systems designed to solve real-world problems. My work includes an administration system for an educational institution, digital platforms for radio and ministry, a barbering and booking application, and experiments with locally running AI assistants.

I am also the founder and operator of The Royals Barbers_CK, a mobile barbering brand built around precision, convenience, and personal service.

Cybersecurity is one of my key areas of growth. I am continually strengthening my understanding of system security, vulnerabilities, secure configurations, and the technologies that keep digital systems reliable.

I believe in learning by building, solving problems methodically, documenting my work, and improving with every project.
