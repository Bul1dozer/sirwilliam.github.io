About Me

I'm Liam, a passionate IT student and entrepreneur with a unique blend of technical expertise and creative flair.

As a freelance IT technician, I specialize in PC and phone repair, networking solutions, and cybersecurity fundamentals. My journey in technology began with a curiosity about how things work, which evolved into a passion for building and securing digital systems.

Parallel to my tech career, I run The Royals Barbers_CK, a mobile barbering service where precision meets personal care. This venture allows me to express my creativity and connect with people on a personal level.

Whether I'm debugging code or crafting the perfect fade, my approach is always the same: attention to detail, commitment to excellence, and a dedication to my craft that goes beyond the ordinary.
